Why it is not the attention banner (`_banner.html`): that banner is DERIVED FROM LIVE STATE on every render and is part of the dashboard's state panel, which a live refresh swaps. This is a fact about what JUST HAPPENED. The page's context POPS it out of the session (`pages/actions. pop_notice`), so a reload — or the next live refresh — does not repeat it. Absent when there is nothing to say: `notice` is undefined on a page that has no write, and `None` when nothing just happened. No empty box, no placeholder — a console that reports nothing is the healthy state. -#} {% if notice is defined and notice %}
