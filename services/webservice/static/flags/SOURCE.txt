Flag and globe SVG assets for the console's language control.

  globe.svg  — Feather Icons (https://github.com/feathericons/feather), MIT licence.
               MODIFIED: the vendored Feather globe used stroke="currentColor", which resolves to
               BLACK inside an <img> (currentColor has nothing to inherit there). It now carries
               EXPLICIT colours — a pale disc (#dbe4ee) and dark slate lines (#334155) — so it is
               legible on both a light and the console's dark (#0b0f14) background. Geometry is
               otherwise the Feather globe unchanged.
  de.svg     — flag-icons (https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), MIT licence. Unchanged.
  es.svg     — flag-icons (https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), MIT licence. Unchanged.
  ru.svg     — flag-icons (https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), MIT licence. Unchanged.
  cn.svg     — flag-icons (https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), MIT licence. Unchanged.
  tr.svg     — flag-icons (https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), MIT licence. Unchanged.
  fr.svg     — the French tricolour, drawn here as three plain rectangles (no third-party artwork,
               no licence to carry): the same 640x480 frame and colours as the flag-icons set so it
               sits level with its neighbours.
  it.svg     — the Italian tricolour, drawn the same way (three plain rectangles, 640x480).
  pt.svg     — the Brazilian flag, drawn here as its three plain shapes (green field, yellow
               rhombus, blue disc) — the stars and the motto band are left out on purpose, because at
               20px they are noise and hand-drawing them invites mistakes.
  pl.svg     — the Polish flag: two plain horizontal halves (white, crimson), 640x480.

Add a language by dropping its SVG here and naming it in LANGUAGE_FLAGS
(services/webservice/i18n.py); a language without an asset falls back to the globe.
